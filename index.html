07 / FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Message me on WhatsApp or Instagram.

How does online coaching work?

You get your own training plan, regular check-ins and feedback on your form from the videos you send, wherever you train. We set up the details together on WhatsApp.

Do you train in person?

Mainly online. In-person training and sports massage in Riyadh may be possible by request. Message me on WhatsApp and we'll see what works.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Everyone starts somewhere. We begin with the basics, learn good technique, and build up at a pace that suits you.

What should I bring to an in-person session?

If we meet in person in Riyadh: comfortable training clothes, training shoes, a water bottle, and any questions you have. For sports massage, loose, comfortable clothes are ideal.

Can sessions fit around prayer times and my work week?

Yes. Online coaching fits around your own schedule. If we arrange in-person sessions in Riyadh, we plan them around prayer times and your working week (Sunday to Thursday).

How much does it cost?

Prices are shared on WhatsApp. Message me and I'll send the options that fit you.

What's the difference between Train + Recover and booking separately?

Train + Recover puts personal training and sports massage into one plan, so your recovery is planned around your training. It's in person in Riyadh, by request on WhatsApp.

How do I pay, and can I cancel or move a session?

Payment and cancellation details are shared on WhatsApp when we set things up.
